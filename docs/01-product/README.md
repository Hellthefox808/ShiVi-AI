# Product Information Architecture & User Journeys
## Canonical 10 Application Modules

ShiVi organizes the complete B2B Revenue & GTM lifecycle into 10 cohesive application modules:

---

### 1. Overview & Command Center
- **Enterprise Scorecard**: Composite readiness, reliability, security, and governance metrics.
- **ARR & Velocity Tracking**: Real-time committed forecast ($1.54M), pipeline velocity ($42.5K/day), and win-rate analytics.

### 2. Prospecting
- **Leads & Contacts**: Firmographic filters, tech-stack identification, and dynamic ICP matching.
- **Company Discovery**: Intent signal ingestion and automated list segmentation.

### 3. Intelligence
- **Account 360**: Unified graph combining CRM data, product telemetry, and support history.
- **Buying Committee Mapping**: Multi-threaded stakeholder identification (Champion, Economic Buyer, Evaluator, Blocker).

### 4. Outreach
- **Multichannel Campaigns**: Context-aware email drafting, LinkedIn messaging, calling scripts, and SMS.
- **Consent & Policy Compliance**: Hard enforcement of Opt-Out, GDPR consent, and sending rate limits.

### 5. Automation & Workflow Builder
- **Visual Canvas**: Drag-and-drop DAG builder with triggers, conditions, AI agent actions, approval gates, and timeouts.
- **Durable Execution**: Checkpointed step transitions with LIFO reverse compensation rollback.

### 6. Verification
- **Deliverability Suite**: MX record lookup, SMTP mailbox handshake verification, and domain reputation scoring.

### 7. Pipeline & Deal Operations
- **4-Stage Kanban**: Discovery ➔ Proposal / Price Quote ➔ Negotiation ➔ Closed Won.
- **Deal Risk Scoring**: Continuous detection of stage stagnation (>21d), missing economic buyers, and communication gaps (>14d).

### 8. Analytics & Reporting
- **Revenue Attribution**: Multi-touch campaign attribution, sales velocity decomposition, and rep quota performance.
- **Executive Forecasting**: Probabilistic ARR forecast rollups and confidence intervals.

### 9. AI Intelligence Hub
- **Executive Decision Anomaly Solver**: Answers *"Why is forecast down?"* with root-cause driver decomposition.
- **38 Specialized Agents Directory**: Complete roster of domain-specific agents with scoped permissions.

### 10. Organization & Settings
- **Identity & Access**: SSO integration (Google OIDC, Okta, Azure Entra, SPIFFE SVID), RBAC/ABAC role assignment.
- **Audit & Compliance**: Cryptographic SHA-256 evidence logs and CSV/JSON export.
